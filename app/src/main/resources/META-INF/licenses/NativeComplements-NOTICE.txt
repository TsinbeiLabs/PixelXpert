Adapted implementations:
- StatusBarOffset: content-only vertical positioning; integrated with PixelXpert preferences.
- RotationSuggestion / LauncherRotationSuggestion: native proposal ownership and
  presentation principle; PixelXpert styles the native button instead of replacing
  its lifecycle or adding the original cross-process proposal bridge.
- NotificationWindow and SystemUiHost.notificationForegroundPackage: notification
  body-click admission and exclusion of floating-window foreground tasks;
  PixelXpert sends native AOSP freeform ActivityOptions instead of using the custom engine.
- SignalDisplay.installStacked and StackedSignalDisplay: module-owned Compose revision,
  reversible presentation-copy identity tracking and caller deoptimization;
  PixelXpert applies visibility rules without modifying native signal levels or DualSim IDs.
- IconArchive and IconLayerModule: dual-layer package archive format, data-only import,
  per-package icon replacement and launcher cache invalidation. PixelXpert normalizes
  layers and integrates a read-only provider and its existing preference framework.
